AI-Powered Engine

How AI Powers ALPHARIA

From the moment a student reads aloud, our AI engine works in real time to analyse, recommend, and track growth with clinical precision.

Step 01
Student Reads Aloud
The student reads a levelled passage while our speech engine records every word in real time — no special hardware needed, just a microphone.
Step 02
AI Analyses the Reading
Our model identifies every error type — substitutions, omissions, insertions — and calculates fluency speed (WPM) and oral reading accuracy.
Step 03
AI Recommends Interventions
Based on the error profile, ALPHARIA surfaces targeted phonics lessons, vocabulary cards, and comprehension activities — personalised per student.
Step 04
Tracks Growth Over Time
Every session is logged. Teachers and parents see longitudinal growth charts showing reading level progression, fluency gains, and error reduction.
